✨ Grammar Cheat Sheet: The Tense Trio ✨

🕰️ Present Simple

Formula: Subject + V1 (add -s/-es for he/she/it)

When to use it: For habits, daily routines, general facts, and permanent situations.

Examples:

  • Fact: Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius.
  • Routine: I wake up at 7 AM every day.
  • Negative: She doesn't like spicy food.

⏪ Past Simple

Formula: Subject + V2 (verb-ed or irregular past)

When to use it: For actions that started and finished completely in the past. Usually accompanied by time words like yesterday, last week, in 2015, two days ago.

Examples:

  • Completed Action: We visited the museum yesterday.
  • Irregular Verb: He went to the store two hours ago.
  • Question: Did you finish your homework?

🌉 Present Perfect

Formula: Subject + have/has + V3 (Past Participle)

When to use it: For past actions connected to the present! This includes life experiences, actions that recently finished (often with just, already, yet), or actions that started in the past and continue today (with since, for).

Examples:

  • Experience: I have traveled to Japan three times.
  • Recent Action: She has just finished her dinner.
  • Duration: They have lived here since 2020.
